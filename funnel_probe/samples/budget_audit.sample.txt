budget accounting
  one GAS iteration = 1 NEW point (the proposal) + a re-evaluation of the current point + 1 gradient at
  the current point; with the landscape supplied as two copies that is 6.0 landscape-function calls per iteration, of which 1 distinct new point. Random: 1 call per point, 1 distinct point per call (x2 copies not used: random calls the function directly).
  So 'equal budget' in C20 meant equal NUMBER OF NEW POINTS (max_iters = B), with GAS also receiving a
  gradient at each point that random does not get, and GAS spending FEWER new points when its own
  convergence test stopped it early (iters above).
